## What they are and what they are not

<!-- seccion: que-son -->

The showcase publishes **13 agents**, each with its technical sheet: promise, figures with
provenance, limits, "never" and the drawn process. They are work systems that run on **Claude
Code** inside a **harness** —the wrapper that fixes for the agent its function, its sources, its
tools and its input and output contract—, and all belong to **ARKHÉ**, my agentic ecosystem,
which is explained in the agents document.

**Five are sealed** —Power BI Dashboard Builder, CINE Presentations, AI-APPs Factory, Animation
Workshop and Computational Paper Harness— and eight remain in initial state. What they are not:
services with users. They are tools for personal use with deliverables and evidence; the word
"production" I reserve for the 27 agents at Vesting.

during a real one, does not insert hidden text in a document and never reveals current income in
a negotiation piece.

libraries: those are its three declared limits.

## What the thirteen share

<!-- seccion: lo-que-comparten -->

- **Human gates with an exact token.** No agent crosses a gate —turning on a GPU, closing a
  sprint, integrating a sheet— without the person's literal word. Between 5 and 8 gates per agent.
- **Bait.** A control is worth its green only if it has the destroyed case that proves it fires:
  Hiring Copilot has 8 of 9 controls with bait; the Graduate Studies Assistant, 9 of 9.
- **No claim comes out of the model's memory.** It cites the source with its location or declares
  the gap; no figure is invented: it is measured, calculated, declared or estimated, and it says
  so.
- **Published limits and "nevers".** Every sheet declares 3 or 4 limits and up to 5 things the
  agent never does, because an agent that seems to do everything is an agent without governance.
- **Cost in tools: 0 pesos.** They run on Claude Code and open tools; the return is declared as an
  estimate, not as a promise.
- **Recorded decisions.** Between 8 and 17 ADRs per agent.

It is the same rule with which I govern my data and my pipeline: a control that has not been seen
failing, a figure without provenance or a promise without a limit do not get in.
