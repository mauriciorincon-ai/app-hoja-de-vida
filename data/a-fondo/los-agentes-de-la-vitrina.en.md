## What they are and what they are not

<!-- seccion: que-son -->

The showcase publishes **13 agents**, each with its technical sheet: promise, figures with
provenance, limits, "never" and the process drawn. They are work systems that run on **Claude
Code** inside a **harness** —the wrapper that fixes the agent's function, its sources, its tools
and its input and output contract—, and all of them belong to **ARKHÉ**, my agentic ecosystem,
which is explained in the agents document.

**Five are sealed** —Power BI Dashboard Builder, CINE Presentations, AI-APPs Factory, Animation
Workshop and Computational Paper Harness— and eight remain in initial state. What they are not:
services with users. They are tools for personal use with deliverables and evidence; the word
"production" I reserve for the 27 agents at Vesting.

## How an agent is born: the constitution, the commands and the gates

<!-- seccion: como-nace-un-agente -->

The thirteen share an anatomy, and it is worth describing it just once. Each agent is a
repository with a **constitution**: a `CLAUDE.md` file that Claude Code loads simply by opening
the folder and that fixes the agent's laws, its commands and its sources. The ISO 42001 Expert,
for example, has **8 laws and 9 commands**; Cold Attraction, **8 commands** in its cycle (brand,
brand manual, campaign, activity, feedback, pipeline, report and closing); the Tax Expert, **12
commands**; Hiring Copilot, **12 commands over 8 modules**. There is no startup prompt to paste:
the constitution is the startup.

On top of that constitution goes the **harness**: the input and output contract, the allowed
tools, the format of each deliverable and the **binary acceptance criteria** —pass or fail, with
no intermediate judgment— that are counted one by one in the package's work items: 29 in the
AI-103 Super Guide, 50 in the Graduate Assistant, 99 in Hiring Copilot, 20 in the Dashboard
Builder, 33 in the Animation Workshop, 52 in the Design Science Harness and 44 in the
Computational Paper Harness. A criterion that cannot fail is not a criterion, and that is why
each control carries its **bait**: the case deliberately destroyed that proves the control fires.

And on top of the harness, the **human gates**: points where the agent stops and does not continue
until the person types a literal token. CINE Presentations has **8 gates** with a verbal token,
one per cut; the Animation Workshop, **6**; Cold Attraction, **6**; the Dashboard Builder, **5**
human approval gates. The gate is not a reminder: it is mechanics. The Super Guide does not
generate a guide before the coverage map is approved; the Library does not integrate a card
without the curator's token recorded with a date; the Workshop does not turn on the GPU without an
approved script and storyboard.

## The learning agents, one by one

<!-- seccion: aprendizaje-uno-por-uno -->

**AI-103 Super Guide** (initial, v2.1.0). It inverts studying: first a real project in Azure AI
Foundry is defined and the harness proves, with a coverage map against the exam's official
weights, that building it covers the syllabus. Only then does it generate the step-by-step
guides, with the official callouts injected where they are needed; every pedagogical claim cites
its official source with URL and date or is marked "to be confirmed". It has **29 binary
criteria** spread across 9 work items and **6 smart notes** seeded. It executes nothing in Azure,
it is not an exam simulator (five to ten questions per guide) and it delivers HTML files that
open in the browser. It is the agent with which I study my own certification in progress, and
that is why it is in the showcase: it is evidence of how I learn.

**Graduate Assistant** (initial, v1.0.1). Each class session enters a Markdown library with exact
provenance down to the timestamp of the recording or the slide number; that library feeds the
spaced review and the matrix of thesis project options. Every citation is generated from CSL-JSON
with a style processor, never written by hand: the invented citation stops being possible. **50
binary criteria** in 7 work items, **9 of 9 baits** fire, and it transcribes at about 19 times
real time with Whisper locally. It prepares, it does not stand in: it does not write the
student's gradable deliverables; it neither hears nor sees —it operates on what the chain
transcribes—, and it does not enter the program's platforms: the student brings the files.

**Hiring Copilot** (initial, v1.1.1). For an AI professional seeking a mid-to-senior role in
Colombia or in Europe: every sendable claim links an achievement to its source and a program
checks it, and the orphan bullet makes the control fail. **99 criteria** in twelve work items, of
which 16 are met today, with **8 of 9 controls** with bait; a coherence script compares the count
against the disk on every run. It prepares applications and does not send them; it trains before
the interview and never assists during a real one; it issues no ATS score because there is no
common published one, and it never inserts hidden text into a document.

## The knowledge agents, one by one

<!-- seccion: conocimiento-uno-por-uno -->

**ISO 42001 Expert** (initial, v1.0.0). Each client receives a repository stamped from the same
mold: intake, gap analysis with the statement of applicability of the **38 controls** of Annex A,
plan, policies and the monthly cycle the market almost never sells. Every normative claim cites
the corpus with clause and page or declares the gap, and every ruling stamps the date on which its
validity was verified: it is "the expert who knows when it stopped being up to date". It prepares
the **24 documents** an auditor asks for. A single core, the 42001: the AI Act, NIST or local laws
enter as satellites mapped toward it. It does not certify or attest publicly, does not execute
code or read telemetry, and never reproduces the text of the standard: it paraphrases and refers
to the reader's licensed copy. It is the same agent I use in the institutional strategy of
Fundación CTIC.

**Tax Expert** (initial, v1.0.0). It keeps a twin of the user's finances in files of their own,
calculates the estimated tax to date, walks through the legal levers that apply to their case and
prepares what the accountant needs, citing in every claim the article, the source and the
validity. The corpus has **150 citable rules**: 39 on individual income tax, 39 on corporate
structure, 30 on the Simple Regime, 20 on calendar and penalties, 12 on VAT and ICA and 9 on
withholdings. It covers Colombia and the DIAN; it serves a single user, with no server and no
cloud; it does not fill in forms or file before the DIAN, which is a personal act of the
taxpayer; and a suggestion without its cited rule, its assumptions and its sensitivity is not
issued.

**GenAI Trends Library** (initial, v1.2.0). A librarian agent sweeps the census's primary sources
—zero press— and presents up to seven candidates per run; the curator decides one by one with
their exact phrase, and what is approved is integrated as a bilingual card connected to the
graph. **59 canonical cards** in Spanish and English, checked against the 59 nodes of the graph,
and **61 census entries** with a verdict. Only GenAI infrastructure trends —patterns, protocols
and reusable layers—; of twelve commands, two are exercised and the other ten are declared
"unused"; it never goes around a block, a paywall or a login: it reports it and moves on.

## The production and research agents, one by one

<!-- seccion: produccion-uno-por-uno -->

**Power BI Dashboard Builder** (sealed, v1.1.0). It receives the requirements as text and a
project with the tables; it diagnoses the model, proposes the list of measures, columns and
relationships for the person to approve, creates them through the modeling MCP server testing
each measure in DAX, and then writes the entire report in Power BI's native JSON with a skill of
its own, with no CLI and no Fabric ecosystem. **20 criteria**, **5 human gates** and 33 reference
dashboards studied. It builds from scratch (migrating between sources is out of scope), delivers
a versioned local project and does not publish to the cloud service; it cannot see the screen, so
the render is confirmed by a person in Power BI Desktop.

**CINE Presentations** (sealed, v1.5.0). Brief, storyboard with full coverage of the messages, a
motion system of the piece's own, shooting scene by scene and a human gate at every cut; what
comes out is a deterministic web deck —code animation, not video— with its editorial cut for
whoever asked for less motion. The first piece has **24 scenes** choreographed in both branches,
**206 regression checks** and 15 ADR. The method is a hypothesis until the first complete
production closes, and the estimated 50% saving is an assumption of my own, to be corrected with
the real hours.

## What "sealed" means and what "initial" means

<!-- seccion: sellado-e-inicial -->

The sheets declare a status and a version, and both words have a definition. **Sealed** means the
agent closed at least one complete run with its real deliverable, that its gates were exercised
with real tokens and that its version was stamped with a date: the Dashboard Builder (v1.1.0),
CINE Presentations (v1.5.0), the AI-APPs Factory (v1.1.0), the Animation Workshop (v1.3.0) and
the Computational Paper Harness (v1.21). **Initial** means the harness is built and verified, but
its first real run has not yet happened or has not closed: Cold Attraction says so of its eight
commands; the Library, of ten of its twelve; the Graduate Assistant, of its 13 commands with 0
exercised. Publishing that fact is part of the method: a sheet that said "tested" without a run
would be the same figure without provenance that the agents forbid themselves.

The return follows the same rule in all thirteen sheets: **it is declared as an estimate with
assumptions**, never as a promise. The Super Guide monetizes the certification in billable hours
at the user's rate and does not claim a salary jump; Cold Attraction models hours freed up; CINE
and the Workshop declare their saving as a hypothesis to calibrate; the Tax Expert does not
estimate the direct tax saving because it depends on figures that are not yet in the twin, "and
putting a number on it today would be inventing it".

## What the thirteen share

<!-- seccion: lo-que-comparten -->

- **Human gates with an exact token.** No agent crosses a door —turning on a GPU, closing a
  sprint, integrating a card— without the person's literal word. Between 5 and 8 gates per agent.
- **Baits.** A control is worth its green only if it has the destroyed case that proves it fires:
  Hiring Copilot has 8 of 9 controls with bait; the Graduate Assistant, 9 of 9.
- **No claim comes out of the model's memory.** It cites the source with its location or declares
  the gap; no figure is invented: it is measured, calculated, declared or estimated, and it says
  so.
- **Published limits and "never".** Each sheet declares 3 or 4 limits and up to 5 things the agent
  never does, because an agent that seems to do everything is an agent without governance.
- **Cost in tools: 0 pesos.** They run on Claude Code and open tools; the return is declared as an
  estimate, not as a promise.
- **Recorded decisions.** Between 8 and 17 ADR per agent.

It is the same rule with which I govern my data and my pipeline: a control that has not been seen
to fail, a figure without provenance or a promise without a limit do not get in.
