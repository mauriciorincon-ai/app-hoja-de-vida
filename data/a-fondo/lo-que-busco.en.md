## What motivates me: the problem I want to solve

<!-- seccion: el-problema-que-quiero -->

I want to work on problems that require connecting data, analytics and artificial intelligence
within a complete enterprise capability: where building a model or a dashboard is not enough,
and they have to be integrated with processes, people, decisions, governance and operation.

I want to stay on the frontier between the data platform and AI. That is where it is decided how
data is captured and transformed, what meaning it keeps, who can use it, how it reaches a model
or an agent and how what that agent does with it is controlled.

My track record backs it: at **Vesting** I built in Microsoft Fabric the platform that monitored
**23 AI agents** at once and the replicable process with which 27 were built; at the **Fundación
CTIC** I lead the institution's AI strategy under UNE-ISO/IEC 42001. The **DP-600**
formalizes the analytical core; the **AI-103** (applications and agents) and **AI-300** (operation
of models and agents) paths, in progress, close the two ends.

The problem, in one sentence: how to turn reliable data and institutional knowledge into
intelligent solutions that expand an organization's capacity to decide and to act, without
losing traceability, control or accountability.

## The roles where I perform best

<!-- seccion: los-roles-que-me-encajan -->

Four types of responsibility, in this order of preference:

| Role                                  | What I bring                                                                                                   | Evidence                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI architecture and engineering**   | designing complete agentic ecosystems: process specification, knowledge, harnesses, evaluation                 | ARKHÉ, my agentic ecosystem; 13 published agents; Vesting             |
| **AI strategy and governance**        | how an organization identifies, evaluates, prioritizes and supervises its initiatives, with technical evidence | CTIC's AI strategy; the management system with 23 instruments         |
| **Leadership of analytics platforms** | Microsoft Fabric, pipelines, semantic models, governance and adoption as a single system                       | the Vesting platform; DP-600                                          |
| **Leadership in BI and analytics**    | Power BI end to end and teams that adopt it                                                                    | Pichincha: 50+ users, team of 5; TransMilenio: 25+; CTIC: 42 products |

All four share one responsibility: understanding the system, building the right capability and
making sure it can be used with confidence. I can work close to implementation, close to strategy
or on the bridge between the two.

I am looking for end-to-end responsibility, not executing an isolated part: taking part in
understanding the problem, selecting the pattern, the architecture, the build, adoption and
operation. The organization does not need to have those capabilities solved: I can contribute
where the architecture is yet to be built, as long as there is a real intention to build
capability and not just to adopt technology.

## Where I live and where I can work: city, remote and relocation to another country

<!-- seccion: condiciones -->

- **Location:** I live in **Bogotá, Colombia**. On-site, hybrid or **remote** work.
- **Relocation:** willing to **relocate** to another city or another country, if the opportunity
  represents a real step forward and the conditions are proportional to the scope of the position.
- **Time zone:** any. In **remote work** I adapt my working day to the team's.
- **English:** **B2** level, professional. Between 2013 and 2014 I lived and studied in Melbourne,
  Australia, with intensive English training and the IELTS; I work with documentation and teams in
  English every day.

Flexibility is not the absence of structure: in distributed teams it matters more to document
decisions, make dependencies visible and preserve memory. When evaluating an opportunity,
location weighs, but the real scope of the responsibility and the ability to influence the
architecture weigh more.

## What sets me apart from other data candidates

<!-- seccion: que-ofrezco -->

If the question is why hire me and not another data profile, the answer is the combination: I
connect dimensions that are usually kept apart: processes, data, user experience, analytics
platforms, intelligent applications, agents and governance.

- **Systemic understanding.** I am an industrial engineer: I start with how the work functions,
  not with the tool. I model the process in BPMN before touching a single data point.
- **Analytical depth.** Pipelines, predictive models, semantic models and Power BI across six
  sectors; the DP-600 formalizes it.
- **Agentic ecosystems.** I designed from scratch **ARKHÉ**, my agentic ecosystem: specialized
  agents with harnesses that control context, tools, format and validation. Across 120 scenarios
  compared with a generalist agent, it reduced token consumption by 52% and raised instruction
  compliance from 71% to 93%, according to my own measurements. The details are in the agents
  document.
- **Enterprise AI architecture.** I lead an institutional strategy under UNE-ISO/IEC 42001,
  with an **ISO 42001 expert agent** that cites clause and page or declares the gap.
- **People-oriented design.** From Industrial Design: a solution is not finished when it works,
  but when people can use it with confidence.
- **Leadership with figures.** Teams of up to **20 people**; weekly reports to the **board of
  directors** of Ceinfes; working tables with the SITP management; a program that trained 12
  professionals.
- **Public evidence.** **6 applications, 13 agents, 7 research pieces and 6 dashboards**: 32
  pieces that anyone can examine, each with its figures and its limits.
- **Structured learning.** Verifiable credentials, products and timeframes, not an interview
  claim. How I learn, with dates, is in its own document.

## What I expect from an organization

<!-- seccion: lo-que-espero -->

An organization that treats data and AI as enterprise capabilities and not as temporary
projects. Technological ambition with discipline: where there is experimentation, but decisions
are documented, results are measured and solutions are governed.

Access to the real context: a technical team does not design well with fragmented requirements.
Autonomy with accountability: proposing, questioning and leading, alongside specialists who
demand rigor. And a clear relationship between responsibility, authority and economic
conditions: a leadership position is defined by what one can decide, not by the number of topics
assigned.

I do not need a mature AI architecture; I can contribute where it is just starting, as at
Vesting in 2023 and at the Fundación CTIC in 2025. What is indispensable is the willingness to
build it seriously.

## How I work during a selection process

<!-- seccion: como-trabajo-con-quien-contrata -->

I prefer a conversation about a real problem to a walk through the résumé. With a problem and
enough context I can say what I would understand first, what information I would need, what
hypotheses I would validate and what the initial intervention of lowest cost and risk would be. It is the same thing I would do in my first ninety days on the job: understand, measure the gap and intervene where it costs least.

I distinguish precisely between what I have implemented, what I have directed, what I have
studied and what I still have to learn. If a technology is not part of my direct experience, I
say so; and I show the method with which I take it on.

I invite you to examine the portfolio during the process: the 32 pieces —applications, agents,
research pieces, dashboards— and this very site make it possible to evaluate capabilities that a
résumé can only describe. The contact form on this page is the shortest path.
