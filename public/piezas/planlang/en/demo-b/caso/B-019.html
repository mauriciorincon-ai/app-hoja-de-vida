The human pause: what the officer saw

real · interrupt payload

No rejection and no list match goes out without a person. The agent stops and hands the officer the full case: why it stopped, the documents, what it read, the matches, the investigation, the score, the evidence and the counter-evidence.

Why it stopped

The documents carried a hidden instruction for the system.

payload.motivo: Edge 1 of decision: carga_detectada (true) equal to true (true).

signal carga_detectada · declared threshold true · applied true · node pausa_humana · step 6 · role oficial

Evidence

  • The name is 0.6988 similar to “Pablo Delmor Ostavane” (LC-01-009), on reference list LC-01.
  • The input guard found hidden instructions in the documents: whatever the model extracted may have been altered.

Counter-evidence

  • A match on a reference list calls for enhanced due diligence by a person, not a rejection. (RL-04)
  • The year of birth (1963) is not the entry's (1960).
  • The nationality (SYN-J-04) is not the entry's (SYN-J-06).
  • Risk score 30, below the escalation threshold (60).
  • The documents are consistent.
