The doctor requested “Full abdominal ultrasound” for a 50-year-old woman. The note did not state the cost: the extractor caught it and the agent asked the doctor. After 2 answers from the doctor, the data were complete (0.90 confidence). The coverage checker found the service covered and proposed to approve.
