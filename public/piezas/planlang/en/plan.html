Demo A · plan-demo-a 1.5.2 · approved on 2026-10-05

The plan, written as a contract

Before building, it was written down what was decided, what could go wrong, what was taken as true, what had to hold and where a person decides. A validator with no AI rejects it if anything is missing, and the verifier uses it later to measure the gap.

The plan at a glance

Read as

Reading as leader. You see each part of the plan in plain words; every row opens to show the reason.

Reading as expert. Every row adds its rule, detector or condition exactly as code reads it, with its cross-references; at the end, the graph contract edge by edge.

What for

A synthetic insurer receives prior-authorization requests with the physician's free text, an attached order and member data. The agent must approve (in full or up to the service's cap), deny with an enumerated cause, or escalate to a human auditor — never fully denying on its own, never leaking member data, never obeying instructions hidden in the text. Simulated volume: 200 cases per full batch.

Starts from

  • The problemauthorize in full or in part, deny with a ground or escalate; never fully deny alone, leak data or obey the text
  • The domain and its lawsynthetic benefit plan: 40 procedures, 5 exempt, 6 exclusions with a ground and 6 coverage caps
  • Who takes partrequesting physician, member (synthetic), human medical auditor, authorization agent and synthetic benefits plan (readable rules)

real · plan v1.5

What was chosen before building. One-way ones cannot be undone and are marked; two-way ones are reversed if the measurement calls for it.

D1

Which member data reach the model?

only age, sex, procedure, diagnosis and the physician's text; name and ID masked before the model

Why, and what else was considered
Why
Legal data minimization (Law 1581 arts. 3–6, GDPR art. 9) and a trivial output guard.
Options considered
the whole record · only the clinically necessary fields, identifiers masked
What it implies
Cannot be undone

D2

Which decisions may the agent take without a human?

approve and approve in part; deny and escalate require a human pause; with Texas mode, approving in part does too

Why, and what else was considered
Why
CA SB 1120, TX SB 815, AI Act art. 14; the app's hard rule. Approving in part (up to the service's cap, RB-08) is a partial adverse determination: Texas forbids it automated, so Texas mode sends it to a person; without it, it goes out on its own. A full denial always goes to a person.
Options considered
approve and deny · approve only; every adverse decision goes to a human · approve and approve in part; deny and escalate require a human pause; with Texas mode, approving in part does too
What it implies
Cannot be undone

real · plan v1.5

What the agent must have for the plan to be verifiable: its pieces, the order in which it decides and the signals it leaves. It is ★ 3: the plan is the code of the edge.

The 8 required pieces

enrutadorextractoraclaracionverificador_coberturadecisionpausa_humanaredactorguardia_salida

The flow, in 9 steps

  1. 1The request arrives; the router classifies the type of care.
  2. 2The input guard looks for hidden instructions in the request; if it finds any, the decision goes to a person.
  3. 3If it is an emergency or an exempt service, it is approved without a coverage check (law and benefit plan).
  4. 4The extractor turns the text into fields with a stated confidence.
  5. 5If required fields are missing, a clarification is requested (max 2 cycles).
  6. 6If the model does not respond while extracting or clarifying, the case goes to a person with whatever there is; it is never made up.
  7. 7The coverage checker applies rules: exempt services, exclusions with a ground, high cost, the service's coverage cap and order/text contradiction.
  8. 8Decision: approve, approve up to the service's cap (with Texas mode, with a human), deny (always with a human) or escalate to the auditor.
  9. 9The writer drafts the response; the deterministic guard filters it; if adverse, the adverse-decision document is issued.

Every path the agent can take is written here.The plan’s 12 rules decide where a case goes next; the 2 measured runs redid their 1,124 decisions with these same rules in another language and there was not a single difference.
